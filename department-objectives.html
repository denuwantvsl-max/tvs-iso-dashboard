Objectives by Department 7 objectives
01 · 👥 Human Resource
Malindu / Kaushal
2024 objectiveRecruitment completed within 14 days
Initial → target— → 14 days · Six-monthly
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • SPARK — skill entry exam for new assembly hires on the candidate’s phone (20 picture questions, English/Sinhala, scored Advanced / Intermediate / Basic / Not Qualified)
  • HR Carder Dashboard — approved vs actual carder by section, daily attendance
2026–27 update DraftKeep: recruitment within 14 days. Add: 100% of new assembly hires pass the SPARK entry exam (70% or more) before line assignment; 6-monthly SPARK skill evaluation for existing staff.
02 · ✅ Quality
Kalpa / Kaushal
2024 objectiveDirect pass rate +2%
Initial → target90% → 92% · Annually
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • MIS Quality module — daily quality figures on the MIS site and in the Daily / Weekly Pulse card
2026–27 update DraftKeep: direct pass 92%, now measured daily from the MIS. Add: monthly direct-pass trend reviewed at the quality meeting, using the MIS figures as the record.
03 · 🏭 Production
Denuwan / Kaushal
2024 objectiveProductivity factor +10% with two new model variants
Initial → target2.0 → 2.2 · Annually
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • MIS Daily Pulse / Weekly Pulse — production by line every day, attendance and units per person (from the HR Carder data)
  • Command Centre AI assistant — answers production questions from the MIS figures
2026–27 update DraftKeep: productivity factor 2.2. Add: units per person tracked daily per section (CKD / SKD / 3W) from the MIS Pulse; target to be set from the 2026 baseline.
04 · 📦 Stores
Chathura / Kaushal
2024 objectiveUnloading verification + GRN time of CKD/DVA shipments −10%
Initial → target12 h → 10 h · 8 months
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • TVS Chassis Verify — AI reads the chassis and engine number photos at de-packing and checks them against the shipment packing list; mismatches are held
  • MIS Stores module — daily stock in box form; Sales stock site for the sales team
2026–27 update DraftKeep: GRN time 10 h. Add: 100% of bikes photo-verified (chassis + engine) against the packing list at de-packing, 0 unverified bikes released.
05 · 🚚 Delivery
Anura / Kaushal
2024 objectiveOn-time delivery of two-wheelers +5% per month
Initial → target70% → 80% · Annually
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • MIS Delivery module — daily delivery figures and pending vehicles; delivery in the Weekly Pulse
2026–27 update DraftKeep: on-time delivery 80%, measured from the MIS delivery records.
06 · ⭐ Customer Satisfaction
Denuwan / Kaushal
2024 objectiveCustomer satisfaction +5%
Initial → target85% → 90% · Annually
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • No digital / AI tool yet
2026–27 update DraftKeep: 90%. Proposed: move the survey to a phone form so results are recorded and summarised automatically.
07 · 🔧 Maintenance
Hirushan / Denuwan
2024 objectiveOnline maintenance work-order system; no more than 10 requests per month
Initial → target— → ≤10 per month · Annually
Digital / AI tools now in use
  • TVS Horana CMMS — online work orders with approval and tracking, AI maintenance assistant, monthly PM checklists that raise a work order when a check is Not OK
  • Daily plant readings — generator, compressor, water meter by 10:00; AI reads the meter photos
2026–27 update DraftAchieved: the online system is live and 2026 requests stayed well under 10 per month (13 requests Jan–Nov 2026). Proposed new objective: 95% of planned PM checks done on time, and daily readings recorded by 10:00 on 95% of working days.
